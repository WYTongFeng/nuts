I didn't really like online classes at first. The wifi in my hostel kept dropping, and half the time I couldn't hear the lecturer at all. But after a few weeks? Honestly it grew on me. I started watching the recordings at night, pausing whenever the slides went too fast, and I actually took better notes than I ever did in the lecture hall.

Being able to rewatch recordings saved me before finals. Still, I miss the random chats after class, the ones where someone asks a dumb question and it turns out everyone was wondering the same thing. You don't get that on Zoom.
